# vastare

To **lay waste** — the devastation verb, five occurrences spread over four very different
kinds of destruction, each classified below.

## Textual tradition

| TM (Hebrew) | LXX | Hexaplaric | Vulgate | Occurrences |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| קרקר *qarqar* (24,17, a disputed hapax); ענה *ʿānâ* "oppress" (24,24); לכד *lākad* "capture" (32,39); שׁמד *šāmad* "destroy" (33,52); the plague (16,47) | 24,17 προνομεύσει ("shall plunder"); varia | — | **vastare** | 5 in Numbers (full text, verified; *vasta* 14,3 = the adjective "waste", excluded) |

**Oracle-war**: the star of Jacob "**vastabit**que omnes filios Seth"
([[Liber Numerorum/24|Nm 24,17]] — Hebrew *qarqar*, perhaps "shatter the skulls of"; LXX
"shall plunder"), and the ships from Kittim "**vastabunt**que Hebraeos" (24,24, Hebrew
"oppress" — the *venient in trieribus de Italia* verse). **Plague**: the fire of wrath
*vastabat populum* (16,47) — divine, not military, devastation. **Conquest hardened**: at
[[Liber Numerorum/32|Nm 32,39]] Machir's sons *captured* Gilead in the Hebrew (*lākad*) but
*vastaverunt eam* in the Latin — capture upgraded to devastation (the same hardening as
*yāraš* → [[interficere]]). **Iconoclasm**: the conquest command of
[[Liber Numerorum/33|Nm 33,52]] — *vastate excelsa*, destroy the high places (Hebrew
*šāmad*): devastation as purgation of the land's cult. What Hebrew text Jerome read is
unknown; the alignments are ours.
