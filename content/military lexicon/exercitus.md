# exercitus

An organized force that "goes out" (*yāṣāʾ ṣābāʾ*): the tribal host/army (Nm 1:3), the cosmic
host (*ṣəbāʾ haššāmayim*; *YHWH ṣəbāʾôt*), and cultic service (the Levites' *ṣābāʾ*, Nm 4).

## Textual tradition

| TM (Hebrew) | LXX | Hexaplaric | Vulgate | Occurrences |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| צָבָא — *ṣābāʾ* | δύναμις / στρατιά | — | **exercitus** / *militia* | 366 *(draft: 246 exercitus, 35 militia, 91 unmapped)* |

One Hebrew noun, several Latin faces even within a single chapter: Numbers 1 renders it as
*exercitus* (v. 52) and, in the repeated census formula "omnes qui poterant ad bella
procedere," effectively as *bellum* (vv. 20-45) — *ṣābāʾ* "the muster" read through the lens
of what the muster is for. *exercitus* elsewhere reads the host as a standing **army**;
*militia* (outside this chapter) reads it as **military service**, the sense that feeds
*militia Christi* in the Christian reception.
