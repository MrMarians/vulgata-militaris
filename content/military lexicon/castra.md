# castra

Camp, encampment; by metonymy also "army, host." Root ḥ-n-h, "to encamp."

## Textual tradition

| TM (Hebrew) | LXX | Hexaplaric | Vulgate | Occurrences |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| מַחֲנֶה — *maḥăneh* | παρεμβολή | — | **castra** | 193 *(draft: 148 confirmed as castra, 45 unmapped)* |

Jerome renders *maḥăneh* almost exclusively as *castra*, the technical Roman fortified camp,
not a neutral calque of the LXX's more generic παρεμβολή ("encampment, drawing-up"). The verb
*ḥānâ* "to encamp" becomes *castrametari*, "to lay out a Roman camp."
