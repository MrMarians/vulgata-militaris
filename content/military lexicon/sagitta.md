# sagitta

The **arrow** — one occurrence in Numbers, and it belongs to God.

## Textual tradition

| TM (Hebrew) | LXX | Hexaplaric | Vulgate | Occurrences |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| חִצָּיו — *ḥiṣṣāyw*, "his arrows" | βολίς (καὶ ταῖς βολίσιν αὐτοῦ κατατοξεύσει) | — | **sagittis** | 1 in Numbers (full text, verified) |

At [[Liber Numerorum/24|Nm 24,8]] Balaam's third oracle paints the warrior-God bringing
Israel out of Egypt "cuius fortitudo similis est rinocerotis": the nations that oppose him
will be devoured, their bones crushed, "et perforabunt **sagittis**" — pierced with arrows.
The verse is a compendium of divine-war imagery in Latin dress (see [[hostis]] in the same
breath), inside the very oracle whose *fortitudo* and *rinoceros* mark Jerome's
hebraica-veritas revision of the older "gloria… sicut unicornis". The arrow itself is the
one appearance of category-A missile weaponry in the book: Numbers' wars are fought with
sword and formation, not archery — the arrows belong to the oracle's God.
