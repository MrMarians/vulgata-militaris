# considerare

To **survey** — the appraising half of reconnaissance. Where [[explorare]] names the
mission, *considerare* names the assessment: what kind of land, what kind of people, how
many.

## Textual tradition

| TM (Hebrew) | LXX | Hexaplaric | Vulgate | Occurrences |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| תור — *tûr* (13,2.16.17; 14,34.38); ראה — *rāʾâ* "see" (13,18) | κατασκέψασθαι | — | **considerare** | 6 in Numbers (full text, verified; 23,9 — Balaam's oracular gaze — excluded) |

The commission of the spies is given with this verb: men "qui **considerent** terram"
([[Liber Numerorum/13|Nm 13,2]]), sent *ad considerandam* (13,16.17), with the briefing of
13,18 — "**considerate** terram qualis sit, et populum… an fortis sit an infirmus, pauci
numero an plures": intelligence-gathering as a questionnaire (see [[numerare]] for the
enemy-count). The forty days *quibus considerastis terram* then become forty years
([[Liber Numerorum/14|Nm 14,34]]; cf. 14,38, where only the two faithful surveyors
survive). Balaam's *considerabo* at 23,9 is excluded: there the Hebrew is plain seeing, an
oracular gaze from the rocks, not reconnaissance. A note on the Hebrew: the same root
*tûr* returns at 15,39 for the **roving heart** ("that you do not go spying after your
heart and your eyes") — an inner-Hebrew echo of the spy-story that the Latin, rendering
freely there, lets fall.
